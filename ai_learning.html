AI in STEM education

On learning, teaching, and the role of AI in scientific inquiry.

Large language models and AI are becoming increasingly important core tools in industry and research in STEM-based fields. For learners and educators working in STEM, navigating the integration of AI into our practices can seem overwhelming. Much about AI and educational practice is still unknown, but we can figure things out together as a community. In that spirit, this page contains resources for students who want to use AI as they learn and for teachers wondering how to effectively incorporate and manage AI use in their curricula and classrooms. Please feel free to take, share, copy, modify, and make full use of these materials.
